Sakinah Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026

Sakinah is built to keep your worship private. Here is exactly what happens with your data:

  • Your location stays on your device. If you allow location access, Sakinah reads your position once to calculate prayer times and the qibla direction. The coordinates are stored only on your device and are never sent to us or anyone else. You can choose a city from a list instead.
  • Your Quran reading, bookmarks, prayer log, dhikr counts, fasting log, khatm goals and settings are stored only on your device.
  • No account, no sign-in. Sakinah has no accounts, no cloud sync and no in-app purchases; nothing you do in the app is sent to us.
  • Notifications (adhan and reminders) are scheduled locally on your device.
  • We don't show ads, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell or share your personal information.
  • Children. Sakinah is suitable for all ages; we do not knowingly collect personal information from children and no account is needed.
  • Deleting your data. Deleting the app removes everything stored on your device.
