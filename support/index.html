Sakinah Support

We usually reply within two business days: labs.cluno@gmail.com

  • Prayer times look wrong: open Salah → settings and check your location, calculation method and Asr method (Hanafi or Shafi'i). You can fine-tune each prayer by a few minutes.
  • No adhan sound: check that notifications are allowed for Sakinah in your phone's Settings, and that the prayer's bell is set to "Adhan" in the Salah tab.
  • Hijri date one day off: adjust it by ±1 or 2 days in Settings → Calendar to match your local moon-sighting.
